Right to marriage and family

Indicators:

  • Proportion of women aged 20-24 years who were married or in a union before age 15 and before age 18 (SP_DYN_MRBF15, SP_DYN_MRBF18)
  • Proportion of ever-partnered women and girls aged 15 years and older subjected to physical, sexual or psychological violence by a current or former intimate partner in the previous 12 months, by form of violence and by age (VC_VAW_MARR)
  • Proportion of time spent on unpaid domestic and care work, by sex, age and location (SL_DOM_TSPD)
