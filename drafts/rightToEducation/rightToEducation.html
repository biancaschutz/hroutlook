Right to education

Indicators

  • Functional skills

    • Population achieving minimum functional skills proficiency (%) (SE_ADT_FUNS)

    • Immigration status parity in functional skills (ratio) (SE_IMP_FPOF)

    • Gender parity in functional skills (ratio) (SE_TOT_GPI_FS)

    • Socio-economic parity in functional skills (ratio) (SE_TOT_SESPI_FS)

  • Completion rate

    • Education completion rate (%) (SE_TOT_CPLR)

    • Gender parity in completion rate (ratio) (SE_AGP_CPRA)

    • Location parity in education completion rate (ratio) (SE_ALP_CPLR)

    • Wealth parity in education completion rate (ratio) (SE_AWP_CPRA)

  • ICT skills

    • Gender parity in ICT skills (ratio) (SE_GPI_ICTS)

  • Education and training

    • Gender parity in education and training participation (ratio) (SE_GPI_PART)

  • Organized learning

    • Gender parity in organized learning participation (ratio) (SE_GPI_PTNPRE)

  • Qualified teachers

    • Teachers with minimum required qualifications (%) (SE_TRA_GRDL)

    • Gender parity in qualified teachers (ratio) (SE_GPI_TCAQ)

  • Reading and mathematics

    • Children and youth achieving minimum reading and math proficiency (%) (SE_TOT_PRFL)

    • Gender parity in reading and mathematics (ratio) (SE_TOT_GPI)

    • Language test parity in reading and mathematics (ratio) (SE_LGP_ACHI)

    • Immigration status parity in reading and mathematics (ratio) (SE_NAP_ACHI)

    • Rural-urban parity in reading and mathematics (ratio) (SE_TOT_RUPI)

    • Socio-economic parity in reading and mathematics (ratio) (SE_TOT_SESPI)

  • Youth not in education

    • Youth not in education, employment or training, 19th ICLS (%) (SL_TLF_NEET_19ICLS)
